III · THE METHOD

From five years of light to one forecast

01

Five years of Earth

For every 640 m cell, AlphaEarth satellite embeddings for 2021–2025, sixty-four numbers a year, for the cell and its neighbourhood, with past forest loss and how much forest and land it holds.

02

A model that reads time

A transformer reads each cell’s five-year history together with its neighbours and returns the chance of loss the next year. Trained on 800 tiles that touch no exam tile.

03

Frozen, then sealed

Three independently trained copies, each calibrated on 2024, averaged. Frozen before the backtest, never refitted, sealed with a digest before any 2026 truth exists.

The reference is the same network without the neighbourhood, trained the same way: the best of six candidates on the 2024 validation year, so the bar is the strongest simple competitor, chosen before the model’s backtest.
