## 1. Claim

We forecast, for every 640 m cell with forest in the Amazon + Cerrado region, the probability that
forest loss of at least 2.5% of the cell (about 1 ha) happens in **2026**, using data up to and
including **2025**. The forecast is locked before the producer publishes 2026 loss.

Secondary: the probability that the cell's embedding changes by more than the task 1.8 threshold
between 2025 and 2026.

## 3. Event definitions

- **Forest-loss event** (primary): `forest_lost(Y) ≥ 0.025`, among cells with at least half land,
  `forest_start(Y) ≥ 0.10`, and a valid embedding in `Y − 1`.
- **Embedding change event** (secondary): the per-class rule of task 1.8,
  `configs/thresholds/amazon_cerrado_v1.json`,
  `0faabd120fd0e4857eff2c277131790e9b55f713b859baf29928fda805888226`: 12 k-means classes of the
  starting year's embedding, each with its own threshold at the 99th percentile of change on
  label-stable training cells, 2020–2023. Evidence: `docs/phase1/THRESHOLD.md`.

Both definitions are fixed. If either changes, this issue is void and a new one is written.

## 4. Primary metric and win condition

- **Metric:** average precision (AP) of the forest-loss forecast, horizon 1, level 6.
- **Exam:** target year 2026 on the **test and held-out tiles together** (499 tiles), graded on
  the first Global Forest Change release that includes 2026.
- **Reference:** the best of B1–B4 by AP on validation tiles, target 2024, in the baseline report
  listed in the bundle. That is **B4** (gradient-boosted trees): AP 0.2459, against 0.2000 for B3
  and 0.0895 for B1. B5 is added to the candidates only if it is built and scored on validation
  before the model is first scored on validation; otherwise the reference stays B4.
- **Win condition** — all three must hold:
  1. the model's AP is at least **20%** higher than the reference's on the exam tiles;
  2. the lower end of the 95% interval of that relative improvement is above **10%**;
  3. the relative improvement is **positive on the test tiles alone and on the held-out region
     alone** (guard, accepted by the owner on 2026-11-15; reason in section 9).
- **Interval:** paired block bootstrap over tiles, 2,000 draws, seed 20261115, every eligible cell
  used (no subsampling).

## 5. Secondary metrics (reported, no claim)

For both tasks, every set: precision in the top 0.1%, 1% and 5% of cells; Brier score; log loss;
reliability in ten equal-mass bins and expected calibration error; fractions skill score at 1, 3
and 9 cells; AP skill against every baseline. Separately for test tiles and held-out region. The
backtest (target 2025, same tiles) is reported once per model family; every run that reads the
2025 test truth is logged in `docs/phase1/test_touch_log.jsonl`.

## 6. Baselines

Defined in `src/almanac/baselines/features.py`, scored by `tools/phase1/baseline_report.py` with
`configs/baselines/amazon_cerrado_v1.json`
(`d02ee05b5f2e0bf3ba3d1ab8213f07032b54cef0aedb13b2a1f270d43122b316`), at the code commit listed
in the bundle:

| ID | Score |
|---|---|
| B0 | Constant (training base rate) |
| B1 | Great-circle extrapolation; its change score equals B2's exactly (tested) |
| B2 | Last year's embedding change `δ(Y−2, Y−1)` |
| B3 | Forest-loss task: forest lost in `Y−3..Y−1`, summed with weights `1 − d/5 km`; embedding task: the same on change events |
| B4 | Gradient-boosted trees on past change, neighbourhood change and loss at three radii, B3's scores, forest and land share, and the 64 embedding values of `Y−1` |

B1–B3 are mapped to probabilities by isotonic regression fitted on training tiles, 2019–2023. No
drivers (roads, slope, protection) are used: task 1.6 is parked. The locked forecast package
includes all five baselines' forecasts for 2026, made by this code, so they are graded on the same
truth as the model.

## 8. Grading

- Truth: the first Global Forest Change version with 2026 loss; same sampling (20 × 20 points per
  cell, nearest neighbour), same forest definition, forest at the start of 2026 computed from the
  v1.13 files in the manifest.
- Grader: the evaluation code at the code commit in the bundle (`src/almanac/evaluate/`),
  applied to the locked package.
- Coverage: all 499 exam tiles. Cells without a valid 2025 embedding are not eligible.
- One grade, final when the release is published.

## 9. What would count as failure, and known limits

- Failure: the win condition is not met. The result is published with the same prominence as a
  win, and the reference baseline's map is offered as the product (blueprint 3.11).
- **Limit of the interval method, and why the guard exists.** In simulation
  (`docs/phase1/bootstrap_coverage.json`), the tile bootstrap's 95% interval covers the population
  AP only 65% of the time with 60 tiles and 83% with 240, and errs high: it overstates skill when
  few tiles carry the signal. For the relative improvement it covered 70% at 60 tiles and 97.5%
  at 240. The exam has 499 tiles, but forest loss concentrates in fewer of them. Requiring the
  improvement to hold in both parts of the exam guards against a win carried by a few tiles.
- 2024, the validation year, had about 1.5 times the usual loss (drought and fire). The reference
  is chosen on an unusual year. On validation 2023, B4 is also best (AP 0.2159 against 0.1294).
- The embedding change rate drifts upward from 2020 to 2025 (THRESHOLD.md); the secondary task's
  base rate in 2026 is uncertain by about a factor of two.
- Overlapping tiles of neighbouring UTM zones hold some ground twice; such cells count twice.
