# Pre-registration 2027.01 — amendment 2: a stable training recipe

Written 2026-11-17, before any model under this amendment was trained. Kept as a separate,
separately timestamped file (`docs/prereg/proofs/2027.01.amendment-2.*`), like amendment 1.
Sections 1–9 of the pre-registration are unchanged: task, data, split, event definitions, win
condition and grading stay as stamped. Amendment 1 stays in force except where this text says
otherwise.

## 1. Why

Amendment 1's first run, B5, improved steadily to a validation 2024 forest-loss AP of **0.2816** at
step 30,000, then degraded: by step 56,000 it was between 0.08 and 0.20, and the **training** loss
rose with it (forest term 0.110 → 0.150), so this was an optimisation failure, not overfitting.

The cause was found on training data only, by comparing the checkpoint at step 30,000 with a later
one on the same training patches (`docs/phase2/STATUS.md`, "Diagnosis"):

- In blocks 4–7, attention logits reached 66,000–270,000, and 98–99% of attention rows put more
  than 99% of their weight on a single year (mean entropy 0.00–0.20 of a possible 1.79). At that
  size, bfloat16 rounding alone is hundreds of logit units.
- Weight norms of the attention projections kept growing (no weight decay).
- The embedding head was ruled out: its predicted spread never reached the floor and its errors
  stayed calibrated.

This is attention-logit growth, a known instability of transformers with unbounded query and key
projections (Dehghani et al. 2023, "Scaling Vision Transformers to 22 Billion Parameters";
Wortsman et al. 2023, "Small-scale proxies for large-scale Transformer training instabilities").

## 2. Changes (model family "A2")

Exactly three changes to amendment 1's recipe, all taken from that literature and none chosen by
looking at validation data:

1. **Query-key normalisation.** Queries and keys are layer-normalised per head before their dot
   product (`QKNormAttention` in `src/almanac/model/net.py`), which bounds the logits.
2. **Weight decay 0.05** on weight matrices (AdamW); norms, biases and learned tokens are not
   decayed.
3. **Linear warm-up over 2,000 steps** for both schedules. Schedule (a) is then constant at
   1.1e-4; schedule (b) peaks at 3e-4 and decays by cosine to zero, as before.

Everything else is unchanged: size M, inputs, losses and weights, sampling, augmentation, batch
and step rules, validation procedure, checkpoint selection, ensemble of three seeds, no refit.

Added for monitoring only: at every validation check, the largest attention logit and the lowest
mean attention entropy over blocks are logged on 16 fixed **training** patches. They are
reported; they do not select anything.

Configurations: `configs/model/stage_a2_b5.json`, `stage_a2_m_a.json`, `stage_a2_m_b.json`
(digests in this amendment's bundle). The batch and step rules of amendment 1 are re-applied to
the A2 network by measurement before its first run; if they change a configuration, the change and
the measurement are recorded in `docs/phase2/STATUS.md` before that run starts.

## 3. Order of runs

1. Amendment 1's B5 run completes its 100,000 steps as specified. Its kept checkpoint is its best
   validation check, as amendment 1 says. **Model M under amendment 1 is not run**: it was
   stopped before it started, because its recipe was shown to be unstable.
2. B5 under A2 (spatial mixing removed, schedule (a), seed 1).
3. Model M under A2, schedules (a) and (b), seed 1 each; choice by amendment 1's rule.
4. The chosen schedule with seeds 2 and 3.

## 4. The reference

The reference is the best on validation 2024 forest-loss AP among B1–B4, **B5 under amendment 1**
(its kept checkpoint) and **B5 under A2**. Amendment 1's B5 result is kept: removing it because
its run later collapsed would lower the bar after the fact. At the time of writing, B5 under
amendment 1 scores 0.2816 against B4's 0.2459, so the reference is at least that B5.

## 5. No further recipe changes

There is no amendment 3 to the training recipe for issue 2027.01. If the A2 runs are also
unstable or do not beat the reference, the result is reported as it is, and the plan's fallback
applies: the forecast of the best baseline is locked on 15 January 2027 (blueprint 16.4). Every
run made under amendments 1 and 2 is reported, including the collapsed one.
