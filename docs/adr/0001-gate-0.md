# ADR 0001: Gate 0 — proceed to Phase 1, and replace the threshold rule

Date: 2026-11-15        Status: accepted (by the project owner, 2026-11-15)

## Context

Gate 0 asks whether yearly change in the embeddings is real and stands clear of noise. It passes
if, at both change sites, the share of cells above `τ` in the event year is at least five times the
stable-site share, and the changed cells line up with change visible in imagery. If it fails at
every level the project stops. Evidence: [docs/phase0/REPORT.md](../phase0/REPORT.md).

- With `τ` as the pooled 99th percentile of two stable sites (0.0149 at 10 m, 0.0124 at 640 m),
  the share above `τ` in the event years is 84–100% at the city edge and 34–80% at the
  deforestation front, against 1% at the stable sites. The ratio is far above five at 10 m, 80 m
  and 640 m.
- Embedding change ranks the cells that changed in Sentinel-2 imagery with an area under the curve
  of 0.76–0.94 at the change sites and about 0.5 at the controls. The change maps sit on the
  construction and the clearings.
- The same `τ` is exceeded by 86% of farmland cells at the city-edge site two years before
  construction began, and by a third of the deforestation block in quiet years.

## Decision

1. Gate 0 passes. Proceed to Phase 1.
2. The threshold rule of blueprint section 3.4 (one `τ` per level from stable reference sites) is
   withdrawn. Task 1.8 will choose between a threshold per land-cover class, a threshold per cell
   from that cell's own history, and a single higher threshold, using the per-tile change
   histograms from the streaming pass. Until then, `δ > 0.2` at level 0 is the provisional
   definition of a change event for patch selection.
3. Difference encoding is not adopted for the patch store, subject to one confirming measurement
   on a full tile in task 1.1.
4. A tile is identified by UTM zone and south-west corner, not by the supplier's image id.

## Alternatives considered

- **Pass the gate and keep the threshold rule.** Lost because nearly all managed land would be
  labelled "changed" every year. The primary task (which cells change) would have a base rate near
  one on farmland and near zero in deserts, and a model could score well by learning land cover.
- **Fail the gate because the criterion is not discriminating.** Lost because the question behind
  the gate is answered yes by the imagery comparison. The flaw is in how the threshold was to be
  set, not in the data.
- **Argue the opposite: the signal is crop state and scene date, not land-use change.** Partly
  true and not yet answered. The imagery measure also responds to crop state, so agreement between
  the two does not separate lasting change from seasonal change. What speaks against it: large
  change (`δ > 0.2`) rises three-fold at the city edge and about ten-fold at the deforestation
  front in the event years, and stays near zero at both controls. Independent labels in Phase 1
  (forest loss, built-up gain) are the proper test.

## Consequences

- Easier: Phase 1 can start; the reader, identifiers and quality gates are tested on real data.
- Harder: the label for the primary task is not settled, and the win condition depends on it. The
  pre-registration document cannot be written until task 1.8 closes.
- Watch: whether the 2025 layer differs systematically from earlier years (whole-block shift at
  the deforestation site); year-to-year variation in the noise floor; a streaming pass 2.4 times
  larger than planned.

## Revisit when

- Task 1.8 has per-tile change histograms for the planet.
- Independent labels disagree with embedding change at L6 (task 1.4).
- More than two stable biomes have been measured.
